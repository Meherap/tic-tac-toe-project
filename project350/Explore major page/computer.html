Majors in Computer and Information Sciences

Computer Science

Description: Explore algorithms, programming, and software development.

Average Salary: $100,000

Field Opportunities: Software engineering, AI development, data science.

Information Technology

Description: Focus on managing computer systems and networks.

Average Salary: $85,000

Field Opportunities: IT administration, cybersecurity, cloud computing.

Cybersecurity

Description: Learn to protect systems from cyber threats.

Average Salary: $95,000

Field Opportunities: Security analysis, ethical hacking, incident response.

Software Engineering

Description: Design, develop, and maintain software systems.

Average Salary: $110,000

Field Opportunities: Application development, systems architecture, QA engineering.

Data Science

Description: Extract insights from large datasets.

Average Salary: $120,000

Field Opportunities: Data analytics, machine learning, business intelligence.
