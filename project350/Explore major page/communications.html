Majors in Communications and Journalism

Broadcast Journalism

Description: Report and produce news for radio, television, and digital media.

Average Salary: $50,000

Field Opportunities: News stations, media production, journalism.

Public Relations

Description: Build and maintain a positive public image for organizations.

Average Salary: $60,000

Field Opportunities: PR agencies, corporate communication, branding.

Digital Communications

Description: Focus on creating content for digital platforms.

Average Salary: $65,000

Field Opportunities: Digital marketing, content creation, social media management.

Journalism

Description: Explore investigative reporting and writing for print or digital media.

Average Salary: $50,000

Field Opportunities: Newspapers, magazines, online media.
