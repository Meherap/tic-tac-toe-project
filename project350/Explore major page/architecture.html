Majors in Architecture and Related Services

Architecture

Description: Focuses on designing buildings and structures with aesthetics and functionality.

Average Salary: $80,000

Field Opportunities: Urban planning, design firms, construction management.

Interior Architecture

Description: Combines architecture and interior design to enhance indoor spaces.

Average Salary: $70,000

Field Opportunities: Interior design, commercial design, residential projects.

Landscape Architecture

Description: Focuses on designing outdoor spaces like parks, gardens, and campuses.

Average Salary: $75,000

Field Opportunities: Landscape firms, city planning, environment conservation.

Urban and Regional Planning

Description: Deals with the development and design of land use in urban areas.

Average Salary: $85,000

Field Opportunities: Urban development, policy advising, regional management.
