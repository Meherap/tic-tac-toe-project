Majors in Engineering

Civil Engineering

Description: Design and construct infrastructure projects.

Average Salary: $85,000

Field Opportunities: Construction, urban planning, structural engineering.

Mechanical Engineering

Description: Focus on machines, tools, and systems design.

Average Salary: $90,000

Field Opportunities: Automotive, robotics, manufacturing industries.

Electrical Engineering

Description: Study electrical systems and circuits.

Average Salary: $95,000

Field Opportunities: Power generation, electronics, telecommunications.

Biomedical Engineering

Description: Combine engineering principles with medical sciences.

Average Salary: $92,000

Field Opportunities: Medical devices, healthcare technologies, bioinformatics.

Aerospace Engineering

Description: Design and develop aircraft and spacecraft.

Average Salary: $100,000

Field Opportunities: Aviation, space exploration, defense industries.
